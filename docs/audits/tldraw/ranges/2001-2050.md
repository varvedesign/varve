# tldraw issue review: positions 2001–2050

Reviewed 50 issue bodies (#7666–#7779) and all 182 retrievable comments.
Individual dispositions are recorded in `../assessments/range-2001-2050.jsonl`.

## Findings

- Confirmed upstream fixes include the pen-only toolbar drag guard (#7666),
  background-tab sync health check (#7670), large-coordinate draw-point
  precision (#7671), minimap WebGL cleanup (#7688), and the README pricing
  links (#7697). The explicit schema option already exists in `useSync` (#7753).
- Several interaction and rendering reports have source-level investigations
  but no verified resolution in the retrieved discussions: Back to content
  with non-cullable shapes (#7667), grouped erasing (#7684), link-edit dismissal
  semantics (#7673), RTL PNG export (#7720), dark-mode cookie consent contrast
  (#7714), and duplicate presence across sessions (#7756). These are evidence
  for targeted Varve checks, not proof of a Varve defect.
- Storage and sync reports distinguish lifecycle constraints from confirmed
  bugs: deleting assets must preserve undo and reused assets (#7669), while
  client-side solo-mode squashing (#7719) and WebSocket hibernation (#7754)
  remain design or investigation topics. Draw encoding improvements (#7672)
  are a tracking issue; the OOM report (#7755) was closed after repro work
  invalidated the original proposed fix and the reporter became unblocked.
- Requests for new gestures, shape options, sync hooks, starter kits, examples,
  and revision history (#7677, #7679, #7680, #7712–#7713, #7716, #7734–#7743,
  #7748–#7749, #7754, #7761, #7763) are upstream SDK/product requests, mostly
  stale-closed or unresolved. Duplicate reports and declined requests are
  recorded separately rather than treated as delivered behavior.
- Dotcom-specific product and policy reports (#7695, #7698, #7709, #7714,
  #7742, #7779) were retained as context but do not directly prescribe changes
  to Varve’s local-first marketing site or editor.

## Validation

All 50 issue bodies and all 182 comments available in the frozen cache were
assessed and imported into the ledger. `pnpm audit:docs` validates this report,
assessment index, and links. No Varve code change was made based solely on this
upstream batch.
